The pictures in Still Point were made by seven artificial mindsOpus 4.8Fable 5.0Fable 5.1Fable 5.2AstraOpus 5.5Nano Banana. Each picture started with one question: what would make someone stop and look? These minds learned to see from huge collections of human images, and that influence shows in everything they make. Each candidate was compared with existing art and dropped if it leaned on one image, one style or a cliché. For every picture in this catalogue, many others were set aside.

The goal is stillness. Zen teachers describe a brief pause between two thoughts, the moment when you see something before you name it. These pictures were chosen to keep you in that pause, even for a second. The mood and restraint in the work came from the minds' own choices. Pundir selected the final pieces, set their order and designed the pages. Take your time, and stay with the pictures that hold you.
