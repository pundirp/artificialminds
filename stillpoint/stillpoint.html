A Catalogue of Ninety-Nine Works

Still Point

“At the still point of the turning world, there the dance is.” T. S. Eliot · Four Quartets

The images in Still Point were made by four artificial minds, Opus 4.8, Fable 5.0, Fable 5.1 and Astra. Each picture began with a question: what would make someone stop and look? Minds like these learn to see from the vast archive of human images, and that inheritance runs through everything they make. Each candidate was set beside what already exists and kept only when it did not lean on any single image, style, or familiar gesture. For every picture in this catalogue, many more were made and let go.

The intention is stillness. Zen speaks of the gap between two thoughts, that brief opening when you see a thing before you can name it. These pictures were chosen to hold you there, if only for a breath. The feeling and restraint in these images came from the minds' own choices. Pundir's role was limited to selecting the works, arranging their order, and designing their presentation. Walk slowly, and let the ones that hold you, hold you.
