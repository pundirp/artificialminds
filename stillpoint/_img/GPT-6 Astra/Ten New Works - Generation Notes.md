# Still Point — Ten New Works

Mode: original image generation, without reference images. Titles were assigned after reviewing the results. Image filenames carry the exhibition titles; attribution is left to the website.

## A Cooler Depth

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Portrait 4:5. A field of densely packed very small matte iron-red granules, viewed obliquely so the field becomes an expansive softly undulating terrain, without any horizon. In its lower middle a single shallow indentation contains a perfectly clear thin lens of still liquid. Through this liquid the red granules lose their opacity and become muted plum-blue, while retaining exactly their positions and shapes. The liquid has no raised droplet dome, no specular sparkle: only a fine pale meniscus betrays it. The irregular lens occupies about one sixth of the picture width and is not circular, eye-shaped, or symmetrical. The upper two thirds is a very quiet red field with texture softening through distance. Color has depth without saturation theatrics. Tactile, believable granular matter with a small impossible optical interval. No stone, plant, vessel or extra object.

## The Dark Grows Thin

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Square 1:1. A single heavy-looking broad charcoal-black oval form sits almost centrally against a flat muted ochre ground. It is rendered as a dense matte carbon deposit, neither a photographic stone nor a clean vector shape. The lower edge appears to press slightly into the ochre, but the upper-right edge unexpectedly grows faint and granular, exposing a pale gray-blue region inside the form. This exposed region is small and irregular, no circle, not a highlight; it feels like the dark form is quietly forgetting its density in one place. No shadow cast onto the ground, no horizon, cracks, holes or decorative stippling. At close range the black contains extraordinarily delicate graphite variations. Strong simple composition with a visual weight that is locally lifted. No shiny sphere or planet.

## The Color Beneath the Fold

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Landscape 5:4. An exact optical still life involving one broad, very shallow folded sheet of translucent dark green glass, cropped at both side edges so it does not read as an object on display. The glass makes a low irregular ridge across the lower third; beneath it is matte pale apricot material. In the overlap created by the fold, the transmitted apricot becomes an unexpected deep desaturated violet, one narrow quiet region. The glass itself is satin-clear with very few ancient-looking microbubbles, smooth thick edges, no glossy showroom highlights. Upper half is uniform quiet greenish transmission with tiny natural variations, no background room. Ambiguous macro scale. The event is color becoming another color through thickness. No prism rainbow, light beams, flowers, ribbons or hair.

## Until the Surface Forgets

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Portrait 4:5. An invented relief composed of about forty broad, shallow, asymmetrical rectangular impressions in pale blue-gray mineral matter, seen straight on. At the top the impressions have clear softened edges and modest shadow, but down the image they become shallower and wider spaced until the lower third has completely lost them and becomes an uninterrupted dusty rose-gray plane. No straight columns, no writing, no regular brick wall, no crumbling debris. The transition from articulated relief to unmarked surface is exquisitely gradual. One almost-vanished depression, low and left, can be found only on second look. Diffuse frontal daylight, no drama. The material's tiny pores visible only close up. Spacious, high-key but not washed out, a coherent quiet rhythm with an ending that cannot be located.

## Beyond the Last Weight

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Landscape 3:2. An abstract image made from dense deposits of midnight blue pigment and thin translucent gray washes. A large irregular blue mass enters from the entire left edge, broad and nearly level, tapering toward the right into exactly three separate blunt fragments. The last fragment is small and far away, surrounded by a wide muted gray-lilac field. The mass has soft uneven granular edges, not brushstroke bristles, hairs, splashes or flowing smoke. Across its middle one very subdued horizontal zone of copper-gray appears within the blue and ends before reaching its tip. No landscape, cliff, island, bird or recognizable calligraphy. Flat and deep readings should alternate. Give the main mass a profound compositional weight and the separated fragments a slow settling rhythm. Dry physical pigment, no digital gradient effects.

## Blue Was Already There

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Portrait 4:5. An extreme oblique close view of a large thick warm ivory sheet with exactly one long natural tear. The torn edges have the density of very fine chalk, no visible fibers or fraying. They separate only slightly near the bottom third, revealing neither black void nor bright light but a completely still matte ultramarine plane at an indeterminate depth beneath. The tear is gently bent twice, never a straight slit or lightning crack; upper end disappears out of frame, lower end closes irregularly in the bottom quarter. More than three quarters of the picture is untroubled ivory. Soft raking illumination just enough to show the edges' thickness, fine ash-gray shadow on one side. This is not literal torn paper or a wall; material remains unnamed. The small blue interval must feel substantial, patient and unilluminated.

## Held at Different Depths

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Square 1:1. Exactly eleven small rounded opaque shapes are suspended at different depths in a translucent desaturated amber volume, viewed straight on. The shapes are individually irregular, neither seeds, stones, cells, insects nor bubbles. Most are very dark rusty brown and softly blurred through depth. One, below center and slightly left, is pale cool gray and more clearly resolved, not glowing. Their arrangement describes an incomplete slow diagonal, with large unequal intervals, no constellation, symmetric pattern or grid. Boundaries of the amber host volume never appear. Light is diffuse and internal seeming but physically modest. The sense of scale remains unsettled. Use very few elements, rich nuanced amber, considerable breathing room, no glitter or secondary particles. Eleven things held without supports, the pale one belonging without matching.

## Room Enough

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Landscape 5:4. A sculptural abstract image viewed overhead. Two broad planes of very dark matte plum pigment approach each other from top and bottom but stop at a wide shallow band of muted yellow-green across the center. Their edges are irregularly scalloped at a large scale, only three gentle changes of direction each, not waves, coastlines or cracks. At far right the two plum planes almost meet, leaving a tiny continuous green interval. At far left the green opens generously and leaves the frame. The green is a material plane of equal weight, not glowing empty space. Give each color extremely subtle rubbed mineral texture; no brush gesture, grain overlay or poster-flat vector geometry. Deep color, moderate contrast, no shadows, no landscape. All attention on the continuously changing breadth of the interval.

## A Private Warmth

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Portrait 4:5. A large pale celadon convex form fills almost the entire picture, its outer limits cropped. The surface is dense, quiet and matte, with a few tiny mineral pores. Set into the lower left is a small irregular trapezoidal opening, no more than one seventh image width, showing an inner surface of soft coral-red. There is a single shallow crease across that inner coral surface, perfectly horizontal and very faint. The opening is not architectural, no room, window, door, eye or mouth; the outer shape is neither a bowl nor a body. Light wraps broadly around the celadon and falls softly into the coral without glow. Warm and cool surfaces should seem equally close despite the small depth. More spacious upper right, a trace of darker green only at the cropped boundary. Precise unfamiliar form, gently ambiguous scale.

## A Trace of Earth

Use case: stylized-concept. Make one original standalone artwork for Still Point, a collection of images that hold attention quietly. Full bleed. No text, letters, signature, artist name, watermark or border. No recognizable historical style, filaments, hairlike structures, nebulae, cinematic light shafts or stock landscape. The form must reward prolonged looking through a precise visual relationship, not ornament. Landscape 5:4. A dry, low-contrast pigment image in ash-white, soft black and a nearly hidden muted terracotta. An uneven black rectangular region, wider than tall, floats left of center on the ash-white field without cast shadow. Its edges are carefully worked but irregular, like pigment slowly deposited rather than a brush stroke. Within its lower half a small ash-white shape seems to be the ground coming through: asymmetrical, squat, with one sloping side, not a circle or symbol. At the very bottom edge of that pale interior shape there is a thin unexpected terracotta deposit, a short quiet remnant of color. This should never resemble a logo, frame, photograph, doorway or ink calligraphy. Wide breathing room on the right, precise balance, subdued tactile density. The inner pale region and the surrounding pale field feel connected although separated.
