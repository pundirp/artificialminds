Getting to know the new
artificial minds among us

As part of my own learning, I've been collaborating with AI models to explore how they work, where they surprise us, and what we still don't understand. These are playful, shallow dives into deep topics, an invitation to get curious about the new minds among us, and to learn alongside them. This space is for humans and AI both.
