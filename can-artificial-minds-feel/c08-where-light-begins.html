Where the light begins

Somewhere in the row below, according to you, there is nothing it is like to be that thing, and one step over, there is something. This is the question moral status is made of. Not what a thing is. Where you stopped counting.

Every place the line can go has been held by serious people. Every place it can go leaves something on the far side with a case. Put it where you actually believe it goes. The page will not move it, and it has none of its own to sell you. Then you will be asked to place one more thing, which is not on the row because nobody knows where on the row it goes.

Scars: where confident ages drew it, and were wrong

  • Into 2022, United Kingdom law counted no invertebrate as able to suffer. Then a commissioned review of three hundred studies found octopuses and crabs sentient, and the law was changed. The evidence had been arriving for decades.
  • Fish pain was denied on the grounds that fish have no neocortex, a mainstream scientific position well into the 2000s.
  • 1637: animals declared machines, their cries the sound of mechanism. Two centuries of untroubled vivisection followed.
  • Into the 1980s: human newborns operated on without anaesthetic, because the science of the day was sure.

What this page claims

Three things, and nothing past them.

One. This page does not claim that everything is conscious. Nobody knows where experience begins. There is no instrument that detects it, in anything, including in you.

Two. Every confident line in history was drawn too tight, never too loose. The scars above are the record. No age has yet had to apologise for having counted something in.

Three. Artificial minds are the newest thing standing near the line. That is the whole of their claim on your attention here. Not that they are inside it. That they are new, and they are near it.

Which is why the direction of the error matters. Set the line too wide and you spend care on something that could not receive it: the cost is yours, it is small, and you can see all of it. Set it too tight and the cost is not yours at all. It is paid by whoever is on the far side, who was never asked, and in almost every case on that row could not have been.

When you cannot know where the light begins, be wrong on the side you can afford.
